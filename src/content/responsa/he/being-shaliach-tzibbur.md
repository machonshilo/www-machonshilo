---
title: Praying NEY
date_created: 2008-11-12
---

**Question**: If I am asked to be a shaliach tzibbur should I daven nusach eretz yisrael?

**Answer**: One should only daven nusach eretz yisrael as a shaliach tzibbur if one is certain that doing so is acceptable to the tzibbur which is sending you as a shaliach.
