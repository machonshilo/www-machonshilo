title: What To Do During Hazarath HaShass
date_created: ''
---

Question

How should I conduct myself during the chazarath hashatz of the shaliach tzibbur?
 
Answer

One should obviously not be speaking or communicating in any fashion with anybody else. In addition, one’s feet should be together as they are during the tefillah belachash, and one should be focusing on the words of the shaliach tzibbur.
