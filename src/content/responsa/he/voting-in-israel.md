---
title: Voting in Israel?
date_created: 2008-12-30
---

Question:

I despise the corruption and wickedness of the Knesset and the government of Israel. I don't see the point of voting for such an impure institution.

Answer:

One must separate one's emotions from rational thinking  regarding the issue of voting. If good, pro-Torah, pro-Eretz Yisrael Jews do not vote then we are abandoning the playing field and providing our ideological opponents with more power.
