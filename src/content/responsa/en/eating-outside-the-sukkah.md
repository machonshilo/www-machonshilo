---
title: Eating Mezonoth or Bread Outside the Sukkah
date_created: 2009-09-15
---

Question:

If I go on a day trip with my family during Chol haMoed Sukkot, which involves going to remote areas where there are no sukkahs, must I avoid eating mezonoth or bread?
 
Answer:

When one goes on the type of day trip which you describe one would be exempt from needing to eat in the sukkah, during the day alone assuming your trip is only during the daylight hours.
 
26th of Elul, 5769
