Question
What bracha does one say when putting on one’s shoes in the morning?

Answer
One says שעשה לי כל צרכי which is one of ברכות השחר. Each ברכה is said for a particular action – see TB B’rakhoth 60b, Rambam’s MT T’phila Chap. 7, Rosh B’rakhoth 9:23.
