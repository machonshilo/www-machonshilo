---
title: Making A B'rakha On Building A Sukka
date_created: 2009-09-15
---

Question:

Should I say a bracha upon building my sukkah?
 
Answer:

Yes. In accordance with the Talmud Yerushalmi the appropriate bracha to say upon building one’s sukkah is “asher kideshanu bemitzvotav vetzivanu la’asoth sukkah”.
 
26th of Elul, 5769
