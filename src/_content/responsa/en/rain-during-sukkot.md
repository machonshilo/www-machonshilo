---
title: Rain During Sukkoth
date_created: 2009-09-15
---

Question:

If it is raining heavily while I am in the sukkah, though I am exempt from having to stay in the sukkah, is it considered admirable to remain anyway?
 
Answer:

Just as one is not expected to sit in a room while water leaks upon one’s head, one is not expected to sit in a sukkah while it rains. Not only may one leave the sukkah under such circumstances, but one should. See Rambam, Hilchoth Sukkah, Chapter 6, Halacha 10. [http://www.mechon-mamre.org/i/3606n.htm](http://www.mechon-mamre.org/i/3606n.htm)
